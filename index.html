About Viniyogah

Viniyoga: the right application of what we know

In Vedic usage, viniyoga is the proper application of a mantra or a rite to its purpose. Viniyogah applies the same idea to daily life: ancient practice, understood correctly and lived simply.

Viniyogah comes from a family tradition of Jyotisha and temple practice in Kerala. It is based in Mangaluru.
