The pre-dawn oil bath (abhyaṅga snāna)

In much of South India, Naraka Chaturdaśī begins before dawn with an oil bath. Warm sesame oil is applied, followed by a bath and new clothes. It recalls Kṛṣṇa's victory over Narakāsura.

Questions people ask

When is Deepavali in 2026?

Dīpāvali falls in early November 2026. South Indian households often celebrate Naraka Chaturdaśī with the pre-dawn oil bath, and Lakṣmī Pūjā on Amāvāsyā. Check your local Panchangam for the exact days.

How do I stop clay diyas soaking up oil?

Soak new clay diyas in water for a few hours and let them dry before use. They will absorb far less oil.
