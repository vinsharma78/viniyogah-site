What Navarātri is

Navarātri, the "nine nights", honours the Devī in her many forms. In much of South India the nights are commonly grouped in three: the first three to Durgā, the next three to Lakṣmī, and the last three to Sarasvatī. On Navamī, books, instruments and the tools of one's work are placed before the Devī. On Vijayadaśamī, many Kerala and Karnataka families begin a child's learning (Vidyārambham, Akṣarābhyāsa).

Questions people ask

When does Navarātri begin in 2026?

By the tithi at sunrise in Mangaluru, it begins on Sunday 11 October 2026. Vijayadaśamī falls on Wednesday 21 October. Please confirm with your local Panchangam, as dates can differ by a day between regions.

Which items should be bought fresh?

Flowers, mango leaves, betel leaves and the coconut for the kalaśa are best bought fresh from a local market. Lamps, oil, wicks, kumkum, gōlu dolls and books can be bought ahead.

What is placed for Sarasvatī Pūjā?

Books, musical instruments, pens and the tools of one's work are placed before the Devī on Navamī. They are taken up again with prayer on Vijayadaśamī.
